## About

I’m a Computer Science graduate specializing in Artificial Intelligence, with a minor in Robotics. I enjoy building useful AI products and taking them from data preparation and experimentation through deployment and user feedback.

My experience includes evaluating LLM outputs at Ethara AI, processing large geospatial datasets for flood mapping, and founding TEAM-PSMPV to develop and deliver software products. I’ve worked with 285 GB of satellite imagery in a research project and shipped products including an on-device speech app with over 100 organic installs and a hospital appointment system deployed at Anand Hospital.

## Selected work

### BharatFloodSAR | Geospatial AI and computer vision
Processed 285 GB of Sentinel-1 imagery into approximately 2,400 annotated image chips for an India-focused flood mapping benchmark. Validated data against reference sources and pretrained UNet++ with EfficientNet-B4, Swin-Tiny UNet, and SegFormer B0.

[Dataset and project record](https://zenodo.org/records/7946594)

### OfflineTTS | On-device AI for Android
Built and published a privacy-first text-to-speech app that runs downloaded Piper and Kokoro models on-device. It supports offline use and has received over 100 organic installs. Testing long scripts taught me to handle chunking, language-specific processing, and device constraints.

[View on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.psmpv.offlinetts)

### AppointFlow | Healthcare workflow software
Built and deployed a hospital appointment and queue management PWA for Anand Hospital. It supports staff and doctor workflows, appointment requests, and patient queues. The project taught me to turn operational needs into dependable software and improve it through real user feedback.

[TEAM-PSMPV case studies](https://teampsmpv.com/case-study)

### Anand Hospital digital platform | Healthcare
Delivered a dynamic website with more than 65 pages, an AI enquiry chatbot, and integrated appointment workflows. The ₹50,000 client project gave me experience delivering a production system against real requirements.

[TEAM-PSMPV](https://teampsmpv.com/)

### SENTIENT | Multimodal assistant
Built a local assistant that handles text, images, PDFs, and audio. Working across input types taught me to structure AI applications as modular pipelines.

[View repository](https://github.com/realestshishir/SENTIENT---Conversational-Image-Recognition-ChatBot)
